How to play
  • You travel through five eras: Prehistory, Antiquity, the Middle Ages, the Early Modern age and the Modern age, then home to today.
  • On your turn you get a history question from your current era. Answer it by tapping the right country on the map.
  • Each era needs a number of correct answers (2, 3 or 4, as chosen) before you travel on to the next.
  • You start with 3 lives. A wrong answer, or running out of time, costs one; arriving in a new era gives a lost one back (never more than 3).
  • Lose your last life and the era claims you: a sabre-toothed cat, Spartan warriors, the plague, pirates… You are out.
  • The first traveller to reach today ends the journey; the round is finished so everyone has the same number of turns. Eliminated travellers sit out while the others play on; if nobody reaches today, the journey ends when the last one is out, and the one who got furthest wins.
  • Questions are drawn at random and never repeat in a game.

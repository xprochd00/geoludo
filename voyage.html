How to play
  • On your turn, pick a challenge from 1 (very easy) to 6 (expert). Answer it correctly and your ship sails that many ports.
  • Answer by tapping the right country on the world map. Pinch or scroll to zoom; tiny nations have dots.
  • A wrong answer costs nothing: you just stay in port. Nobody can be attacked or sent back.
  • Compass tokens 🧭: you start with one. Land exactly on a 🧭 port, or answer 3 in a row, to find another (up to 3). Spend one during your question to see which region the answer is in.
  • The first ship to sail around the world back to Lisbon ends the race. The round is finished so everyone has the same number of turns, and the ship furthest ahead wins.
  • Online, each on your own device: while others play, you can guess along. It doesn't move your ship, but everyone sees who knew it.
  • Hazards ⚠️: after a correct answer, the port you reach may hold one: a storm, reef, ice or fog blows you back 1–2 ports, pirates or the doldrums cost your next turn. Easy challenges are risky (35% at level 1), hard ones safe (3% at level 6); each level button shows its risk.
  • Computer captains 🤖 (Rookie, Explorer, Genius) can join: tap a player's Human button, or Add a computer captain in an online room. If an online player loses connection, a bot sails for them after 20 seconds until they are back.

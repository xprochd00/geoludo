    How to play
    • Goal: race your pieces around the board and into the centre. Every move is earned by clicking the right country on the world map.
    • Pick your challenge (Quick): after rolling, choose a question level from 1 (very easy) to 6 (expert). Answer correctly to move roll + level squares. Hard questions get you there faster. In Classic, the number rolled is the level and you move that many squares.
    • Steals & hints: if you answer wrong, the next player can steal the question. A correct steal earns a 🧭 compass token, and so does an expert answer. Spend a token during a question to highlight the right region of the map.
    • ⚔️ Captures: land on a rival piece to send it back. Its owner first gets a rescue question: the further the piece has travelled, the harder the question. 🛡️ Shield squares are safe.
    • Classic: a piece leaves home only on a 6 (no question needed), with 3 attempts when nothing is on the board. A 6 earns another roll.
    • Play with friends on one screen, or add 🤖 computer players. Countries you find are stamped in your 🛂 passport.
    • On the map: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Tiny nations are marked with dots. Keys: Space roll, 1–6 choose, H hint, Enter continue.

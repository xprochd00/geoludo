How to play
  • Players take turns rolling the dice. The number decides the difficulty of the question.
  • The board rolls away and a world map appears. Click the country you are asked about — by its name, capital city or flag. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
  • Correct answer → move forward by the number rolled. Wrong answer → stay put.
  • Land on a rival's square and send them back home!
  • The first to reach the centre of the board wins.

How to play
  • Each player chooses how many pieces (1–4) to race. All pieces start at home.
  • A piece can only leave home on a 6. Bringing a piece out needs no question. With no piece on the board you get 3 attempts to roll that 6.
  • 🛡️ Each player's first square (marked with a shield) is a safe zone: pieces there can't be sent home.
  • ⚔️ Land on a rival piece and its owner gets a rescue question. The further that piece has travelled, the harder the question (see the coloured zones on the progress bars). A correct answer pushes the attacker back to where it came from; a wrong one sends the rival piece home.
  • If only one piece can move, it is picked automatically. Otherwise the pieces that can move glow: click the one you want (or press its number key).
  • Moving a piece already on the board needs a correct answer. The number rolled is the difficulty (1 = very easy … 6 = expert). The board rolls away and a world map appears: click the right country. A wrong answer leaves the piece where it is.
  • The first player to get all their pieces to the centre wins.
  • On the map: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Tiny nations are marked with dots.

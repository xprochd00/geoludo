GeoGames

Geography games on a real world map. Every move is earned by finding the right place: countries, flags, capitals, cities, landmarks, history, sports and culture.

🎲

Geo Ludo

The classic board race, played with a world map. Roll, answer, and bring your pieces home first.

  • Pick your challenge from 1 (very easy) to 6 (expert), then roll: a correct answer moves roll + level.
  • Land on a rival to capture it: its owner gets a rescue question first.
  • Missed questions can be stolen by the next player; compass tokens buy hints.
  • 2–4 players, computer players too · Quick, Classic or Custom rules

Geo Voyage

Sail around the world in 30 ports, from Lisbon back to Lisbon. Choose how far to sail by how hard a question you dare.

  • Choose a challenge from 1 to 6: answer it right and sail that many ports.
  • Hazards lurk in the ports: storms push you back, pirates cost a turn. Easy questions are riskier.
  • Find compass tokens on treasure ports and spend them on hints.
  • 1–6 players, computer captains too · fair finish: everyone gets the same number of turns

Geo Time Travel

Travel through history, from prehistory to today. Find where it happened to move on to the next era.

  • Each era needs a number of correct answers before you travel on.
  • 3 lives; reaching a new era can earn one back.
  • Lose all your lives and the era claims you: a sabre-toothed cat, a legion, the plague…
  • Questions are drawn at random and never repeat in a game.

Training

Practise on your own, with just the questions and the map. Your progress is saved on this device.

  • Choose categories, question types, levels and parts of the world.
  • Missed questions come back a few questions later until you master them.
  • Mastered questions return now and then, so nothing is forgotten.
  • See your progress per category and level.
